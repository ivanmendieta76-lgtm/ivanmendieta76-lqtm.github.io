Privacy Policy — ShaditoPublisher

Last updated: May 2026

ShaditoPublisher is a personal automation tool developed by Iván Mendieta for managing musical content on social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

This application accesses only the social media accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner (Iván Mendieta). No personal data from other users is collected.

2. How We Use Information

Access tokens and credentials are used solely to publish original musical content on behalf of the authorized account owner. Tokens are stored securely in Google Apps Script properties.

3. Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. This is a personal tool with a single authorized user.

4. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as needed for the service to function and are revoked upon request.
